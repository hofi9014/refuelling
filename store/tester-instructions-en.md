Hi! Thank you for helping me test Tankvia – an app that tracks your car's fill-ups, fuel consumption, service and costs.
Google requires 12 testers for 14 days before the app can be published for everyone. It only takes a few minutes.

WHAT YOU NEED
• An Android phone.
• A Gmail account – THE SAME one you use in the Play Store on that phone.
  (To check: open the Play Store and tap your profile picture in the top right corner.)

STEP 1 – Join the testers group
1. Open this link (signed in with that Gmail account):
   https://groups.google.com/g/testerzy-tankvia
2. Tap "Join group" and confirm.
   If you see "Leave group", you are already a member – go on.

STEP 2 – Become a tester
1. Open this link:
   https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.hofitech.tankvia
2. Tap "Become a tester".

STEP 3 – Install the app
1. On your phone, open:
   https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hofitech.tankvia
2. Tap "Install".
   If the Play Store says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes (sometimes a few hours) and try again.

STEP 4 – Start using Tankvia
1. Open the app and tap "Add car": name, fuel type (petrol, diesel, LPG + petrol, hybrid, plug-in hybrid or electric), tank size.
2. The app follows your phone's language (22 languages). You can change the language, currency and units (km or miles) in Settings.
3. Tap the round "+" button to add a fill-up, a charge or a cost.
   • Fill-up: enter the odometer and any two of litres / price / total – the third is calculated.
     Tick "Full tank" when you fill up completely – that gives the exact consumption.
     Tip: "Read from the pump" fills in the amount, litres and price from a photo of the pump display,
     and "Read the receipt" also takes the date, station and fuel from a photo of the receipt.
   • Charge (electric / plug-in cars): kWh, price, charger type and battery % before and after.
   • Cost: service, tyres, insurance, parking and more. "Valid until" (e.g. insurance end) adds a reminder automatically.
4. Reminders tab: a calendar of dates such as the next inspection or oil change. Tap a day or "+" to add one.
5. Dashboard: "Trip cost" plans a route on the map and calculates the fuel and electricity cost of the journey.
6. Reports tab: monthly costs, cost per km, prices by station and a vehicle history PDF.
7. Settings: backup (export / import), CSV export, colour themes, light / dark mode and a magnifier for bigger text.

STEP 5 – Keep it for 14 days
• Add a few real entries and open the app a few times during the 14 days.
• Please do NOT uninstall the app or leave the group / test during these 14 days – otherwise the test doesn't count.
• Feedback, bugs and ideas: hofi9014@gmail.com (a screenshot helps a lot).

COMMON PROBLEMS
• "App not available" or no "Become a tester" button – usually a different Gmail account in the Play Store than the one that joined the group.
  Switch the account in the Play Store, or join the group with the right account.
• iPhone – unfortunately testing works only on Android.
• Updates arrive automatically through the Play Store.

The app is free, has no ads and needs no account. Your data stays on your phone.
Thank you! 🙂
